# Coding-AGI Engineering-Readiness Recalibration

## Why this correction exists
The earlier internal rubric was unintentionally treated too linearly: a large new bounded capability could add many points even when the evidence came from a generated family designed by the same research process. That makes the scale inflate toward AGI without corresponding external transfer. The correction makes later points progressively harder to earn.

## Corrected working scale
These values are internal engineering heuristics, not probabilities of AGI and not scientific measurements.

- R2.49: **39.0/100** (historical anchor retained for continuity).
- R2.50: **40.5/100 provisional recalibration**.
- R2.51: **42.5/100 provisional recalibration**.
- R2.52: **44.5/100 corrected current baseline**.
- R2.53: **no automatic increase from mechanism-only synthetic evidence**. Any increase requires independent transfer that was not used to design the operator/reflex benchmark.

## New scoring discipline
1. Passing CI proves implementation consistency, not intelligence.
2. Passing a benchmark designed with the mechanism proves that mechanism under its declared boundary, not broad transfer.
3. Synthetic/bounded improvements normally earn zero to small provisional movement.
4. Large movements require unseen external task distributions, real repositories/environments, no generator access, and causal ablations showing the new mechanism is responsible.
5. Scores may decrease when stronger falsification reveals overclaiming. New versions are not guaranteed to score higher.

Nolane World W5 remains false, with two fresh verifications but unresolved challenger, robustness, representation-diversity and quality-floor blockers. The **48.5/100** value is an internal engineering heuristic, not an AGI probability.

## R2.62 — 48.8 / 100

R2.62 earns **+0.3** over R2.61 (48.5). It adds a structural capability that is different from R2.61 candidate-space expansion: the runtime can discover a bounded pair of complementary pure-input interventions when neither intervention alone explains the target, reject singleton-solvable pairs, compose the two induced behaviors through a finite operation language, and synthesize the two probe expressions hierarchically under a matched local synthesis budget. The authored benchmark reaches 3/3 discoveries while the matched flat baseline fails 3/3, both proper subsets fail in all three configurations, validation is 30/30, challenge is 24/24, identifier renaming is invariant, argument permutation tracks positional roles, and wrong-pair false accepts are zero.

The pinned external transfer uses `ufunclab.deadzone` at commit `f1fbe6769850823a1976ccc28d14cd966130b645` through callable I/O only. The full two-intervention `add` program is exact on 10/10 validation, 8/8 challenge and 24/24 heldout cases, while the two singleton expressions achieve only 4/8 and 6/8 on challenge, establishing that both interventions are necessary on that distribution. A 10,000-candidate flat direct baseline fails, while hierarchical probe synthesis uses 3,900 candidates under the matched budget. Canonical hosted verification passes 6/6 R2.62 tests, preserves 221/221 R2.61→R2.41 parent tests, recomputes authored and external evidence exactly, and is green on Python 3.11 and 3.13.

The increase remains deliberately small because the external function family is researcher-selected rather than blind, the intervention anchors and composition operators are finite host-provided choices, only two interventions are supported, and all experiments are pure-input. Stateful/temporal/filesystem/network/dependency/concurrency experiments, 3+ intervention scaling, learned experiment-language growth, broad repository coding autonomy and unrestricted program synthesis remain unproven. Nolane World W5 remains false. The **48.8/100** value is an internal engineering heuristic, not an AGI probability.

A host-authored three-file repository initially contains two `Add` sites and only the unmodified repository candidate; R2.61 again terminates after a partial repair, while R2.63 composes two trusted `Add → Sub` mutations and passes 256/256 disjoint final cases. The increase remains deliberately small because the mutation grammar remains trusted and host-supplied, the external repository wrapper is synthetic, and the accepted evidence is bounded to a two-edit repair chain. Scaling to longer chains, safely learning patch-language primitives, effectful/stateful testing and blind real-repository issue transfer remain unproven. Nolane World W5 remains false. The **49.1/100** value is an internal engineering heuristic, not an AGI probability.
