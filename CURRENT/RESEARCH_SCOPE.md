# Research Scope

Current work is the research and engineering of Neural Core, External Core, memory, evidence, learning, verification, organization, and independently evolvable capability structure for 67 permanent AI Identities.

Current scope does **not** include a user-facing Nolane Agent application, desktop/web/mobile UI, commercial product shell, or a claim that the system is AGI/frontier-equivalent.

Real-world capability claims require fresh external evidence under the existing evaluation/claim authority. Repository refoundation improves architecture and auditability; it is not evidence of intelligence gains by itself.
