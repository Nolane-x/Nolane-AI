# FIGG-17 v1.1 reachability correction

Date: 2026-08-12

The first optimizer attempt for R1.7 Causal Law Slots stopped before any gradient step because FIGG-17 v1 exposed a benchmark-generation defect: some `causal_laws` worlds sampled arbitrary goals that were not reachable under their generated conditional modular dynamics.

No checkpoint, optimizer metric, dev task, or fresh task was produced from the failed run.

## Correction

FIGG-17 is bumped to `nolane-figg17-v1.1`. `causal_laws` goals are now generated by executing a deterministic random witness trajectory under that world's own generated laws from the public start state. This guarantees reachability while preserving opaque action semantics and state-conditional dynamics.

## Verification

A new regression solves 48 train + 48 dev `causal_laws` worlds with the exact oracle. The complete benchmark/inherited regression stack after the fix is **45/45 passed**.

The previously preregistered R1.7 law-slot training indices and optimizer protocol remain unchanged; training restarts from the untouched R1.6 EffectProgress parent plus neutral R1.7 law-slot initialization.
