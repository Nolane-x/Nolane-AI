# R1.7 Causal Law Slots — train-only protocol

Date: 2026-08-12
Parent: R1.6 EffectProgress fresh-best `0a1688062f7640739847070a54ea079a28c10c010b286c5b640645214e912ace`
Benchmark: FIGG-17 `nolane-figg17-v1`

## Isolation

Only FIGG-17 `train` tasks are allowed into the collector/trainer. The collector has an automated guard that raises on `dev` or `fresh` tasks.

Families used for Phase-A law fitting:
- `causal_laws`
- `causal_switch`

Fit indices: `0..23` per family (48 episodes total).
Internal-validation indices: `24..31` per family (16 episodes total).
Seed for neural initialization/training: `170117`.
Exploration prefix: six actions where possible, selecting least-used non-submit actions before teacher planning.
Maximum collected steps per episode: 14.

## Inputs and labels

Neural inputs are derived only from public observations and public dynamic action descriptions:
- 128D structured public-state sketch;
- frozen parent action embeddings;
- recurrent law-slot state created from previously observed public transitions.

Counterfactual successor labels for train-only supervision are generated by cloning the train simulator, executing each legal action, and encoding the resulting **public observation** into the same 128D sketch. Simulator internals are never passed into the neural model.

## Baseline

The preregistered baseline is per-action last-observed-effect persistence:
- before an action has been observed, its predicted delta is exactly zero;
- after observation, the most recent public structured delta for that same action is reused.

This baseline is deliberately strong for stationary actions and should expose whether law slots add value on state-conditional and switching dynamics.

## Optimizer scope

Only `causal_law_*` parameters required for law representation/retrieval/delta prediction are trainable. `causal_law_policy_head.*` and `causal_law_policy_scale` remain frozen/neutral during the world-model gate. All R1.6 parent parameters are frozen.

## Internal acceptance gate

A checkpoint is allowed to proceed to FIGG-17 dev only if:
1. aggregate held-out train successor-delta MSE is strictly lower than the last-effect baseline;
2. `causal_laws` candidate MSE is no worse than its baseline;
3. `causal_switch` candidate MSE is no worse than its baseline;
4. checkpoint binds exact R1.6 parent SHA and remains below the 96M hard ceiling.

Offline loss improvement alone is not a capability claim. No FIGG-17 dev or fresh task is opened before this gate passes and the candidate/provenance is pushed.
