# Nolane R2.3 AGI-readiness rubric

This is an engineering rubric, not a scientific AGI percentage and not a probability that Nolane will reach AGI. It exists to keep milestone discussions consistent and deliberately weights broad transferable evidence above internal benchmark saturation.

| Dimension | Weight | R2.3 score | Weighted contribution |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Broad transferable reasoning/generalization | 30% | 10/100 | 3.00 |
| Broad language, coding, math and world competence | 20% | 5/100 | 1.00 |
| Long-horizon self-directed autonomy | 15% | 10/100 | 1.50 |
| Continual learning and adaptation | 10% | 22/100 | 2.20 |
| External knowledge acquisition during cognition | 10% | 55/100 | 5.50 |
| Epistemic reliability / provenance / contradiction handling | 5% | 40/100 | 2.00 |
| Agentic causal interaction | 5% | 35/100 | 1.75 |
| Multimodal perception / embodiment | 2.5% | 0/100 | 0.00 |
| Independent external frontier-benchmark evidence | 2.5% | 2/100 | 0.05 |
| **Total** | **100%** | — | **17.00 / 100** |

R2.3 raises only the continual-learning dimension relative to R2.2. The evidence is strong within the locked restricted-skill curriculum: the system induces transformations from demonstrations, retains unrelated skills after intervening learning, applies revisions, and composes previously acquired skills. The final held-out candidate score was 93.125% versus a persistent replay baseline of 33.75%.

The score remains only 22/100 for continual learning because the demonstrated skill language is bounded, synthetic and arithmetic-like. R2.3 does not demonstrate unrestricted lifelong learning, broad concept formation, transfer across natural domains, or autonomous self-improvement. Broad reasoning, coding/math competence, autonomy, multimodality and external frontier evidence therefore receive no increase from KFIGG-23.

A demanding current engineering estimate is **about 17% AGI-readiness**. Future increases should require new evidence in currently weak dimensions rather than further saturation of KFIGG-23.
