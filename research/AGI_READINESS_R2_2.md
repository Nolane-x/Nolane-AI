# Nolane R2.2 AGI-readiness rubric

This is an engineering rubric, not a scientific AGI percentage and not a probability that the project will reach AGI. It exists only to keep future milestone discussions consistent.

The rubric deliberately weights broad, transferable capability much more heavily than success on Nolane's own synthetic benchmarks.

| Dimension | Weight | R2.2 score | Weighted contribution |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Broad transferable reasoning/generalization | 30% | 10/100 | 3.00 |
| Broad language, coding, math and world competence | 20% | 5/100 | 1.00 |
| Long-horizon self-directed autonomy | 15% | 10/100 | 1.50 |
| Continual learning and adaptation | 10% | 5/100 | 0.50 |
| External knowledge acquisition during cognition | 10% | 55/100 | 5.50 |
| Epistemic reliability / provenance / contradiction handling | 5% | 40/100 | 2.00 |
| Agentic causal interaction | 5% | 35/100 | 1.75 |
| Multimodal perception / embodiment | 2.5% | 0/100 | 0.00 |
| Independent external frontier-benchmark evidence | 2.5% | 2/100 | 0.05 |
| **Total** | **100%** | — | **15.30 / 100** |

A demanding current estimate is therefore **about 15% AGI-readiness**. This is intentionally far below Nolane's synthetic benchmark scores. Future releases should change the rubric only when new evidence changes a dimension; saturation of an internal benchmark alone must not increase broad reasoning or external-evidence scores.
