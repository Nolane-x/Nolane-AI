# R2.61 → R2.62 Evolution

R2.61 and R2.62 improve different failure modes and are intentionally cumulative.

## R2.61 boundary

R2.61 addresses **missing candidate hypotheses** in bounded repository repair. When R2.60 discovers that the oracle output lies outside the current executable version space, R2.61 can expand that space with trusted single-site PatchMacro mutations and resume diagnosis.

## R2.62 boundary

R2.62 addresses **missing causal decomposition structure** in bounded pure-input experiment design. A useful target can require two complementary interventions whose individually induced behaviors are insufficient, but whose verified composition exposes a reusable expression.

R2.62 therefore adds:

1. finite legal single-intervention enumeration;
2. deterministic behavior-profile deduplication;
3. bounded pair enumeration;
4. finite numeric composition search;
5. strict singleton-subset necessity checks;
6. hierarchical synthesis of one probe per selected intervention;
7. matched-budget comparison against flat direct synthesis;
8. validation/challenge/heldout checks separated from discovery;
9. identifier-rename and argument-role perturbation checks;
10. exact external I/O-only transfer evidence.

## Multi-AI integration rule

The capability originated on peer branch `r260-complementary-experiment-program-gpt56sol` at head `92e73ae4af5b060454141e057cda6033d4520a21`. Because R2.60 was already accepted, the peer branch is not merged directly. Its core blob is preserved byte-exact but placed under the R2.62 namespace on top of accepted R2.61.

This avoids version collision and makes the contributions additive:

`R2.60 active diagnosis → R2.61 candidate-space expansion → R2.62 complementary causal experiment programs`.

## What remains open

R2.62 does not solve blind task-family selection, open-ended intervention language growth, 3+ intervention composition, stateful/effectful experiments, or broad real-repository autonomy. Those remain explicit research targets rather than being hidden by the new version number.
