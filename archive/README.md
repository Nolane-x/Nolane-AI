# Nolane AI Historical Archive

This tree contains or receives historical research artifacts after the A1 Refoundation establishes their current authority status.

History is preserved because old R-series experiments, delivery notes, manifests, checkpoint pointers, evidence bundles, and workflow contracts may be necessary to reproduce or audit prior claims. Archive placement therefore means **historical provenance**, not disposal and not invalidation.

`archive/INDEX.json` is the machine-readable inventory for ambiguous historical files that still appear at repository root or have been safely relocated from there. Its entries are generated by `nolane.repository.audit` and always begin with `delete_allowed=false`.

## Wave-4 movement policy

`quarantined_in_place` means a historical file remains at its original location until active references and reproduction dependencies are audited. `moved` means the content has been relocated to its recorded `archive_target` after that audit.

No Wave-4 process may silently delete history, change accepted evidence bytes, or treat archived documents as current architecture law.

For present architecture, start in `CURRENT/`.
